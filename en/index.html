The more you know them, the more you love them.

What kind of day do the animals spend here?

Waking up, wandering about, eating, and dozing in the sun. Sometimes they dive into the water, and sometimes they simply stay still.

The animals don't live by a timetable we set for them. They have their own natural rhythm, changing with their mood and the season.

What they eat, where they sleep, how they spend the day — the more you know of their lives, the closer their world feels.

Meet the animals, and think about the future of nature.

We aim to be a zoo where looking at the animals leads you to think about the nature that lies beyond them.

To know the animal in front of you is also to know the environment it lives in and the changes taking place there. We hope that meeting the animals becomes a starting point for thinking about the future of people and nature.

By creating an environment where animals can live as they naturally would, and by working on conservation and education, we look toward the future of animals and nature from this northern land.
