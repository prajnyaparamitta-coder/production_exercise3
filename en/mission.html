Our Mission

To be a place where you meet the animals and think about the future of nature.

From a Zoo That Shows, to a Zoo That Thinks

Until now, we have brought visitors the sight of the many animals that live in the northern land. Yet simply gazing at rare creatures through a pane of glass may make it hard to notice the wider environment they truly inhabit, or the changes taking place there.

What Hokkaido ○○ Zoo aims for is not merely a place that "shows" animals. It is a shift toward a zoo where we think together — reflecting on the background each animal has come from, and how people and nature should face one another from here. A polar bear swimming in the cold icy sea, the breath of a sika deer living deep in the forest — they teach us the unshakable fact that we share the same planet's environment.

Our Efforts

To realize a "zoo that thinks," we go beyond the daily optimization of husbandry and focus on enrichment that draws out the animals' natural, wild behavior. Creating spaces where they can live vividly and without boredom is an essential foundation for letting visitors feel their natural ways and beauty just as they are.

We also work on breeding for species conservation, and on education and outreach that convey the current state of the northern ecosystem. By sharing the discoveries found in daily observation and unraveling the stories behind the animals' lives, we keep building an environment where a small realization can take root in each visitor's heart.

To Our Visitors

When you pass through the gate and feel the breath of the northern animals, your imagination may suddenly reach out — to the nature beneath your own feet, and to distant worlds. We hope to be a place where a quiet, rich time flows, for grown-ups too.

As a weekend moment with family or someone dear, or as a quiet walk to face yourself — we sincerely hope that a small encounter at this zoo becomes the key that opens a new door connecting you and the future of nature.
